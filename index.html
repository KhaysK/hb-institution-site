About

About HB Institution

Where Knowledge Meets Faith.

HB Institution is the educational arm of HB Foundation, established with a clear purpose: to provide authentic Islamic education through sincerity, structure, discipline, and care.

We believe sacred knowledge should be taught with responsibility. Not rushed. Not diluted. Not treated as simple content delivery. The Quran and Islamic sciences carry weight, and teaching them requires qualified teachers, clear standards, accountability, and genuine concern for every student.

HB Institution was built to create an environment where faith and education work together. A place where students not only learn how to recite, memorise, and study, but also develop character, discipline, understanding, and a stronger connection to Allah.

Every program within the institution is designed with structure and measurable progression. From Quran and Tajweed to Hifz, Islamic sciences, and Arabic studies, students are guided through a clear journey with proper assessment, reporting, and support along the way.

As part of the wider mission of HB Foundation, this institution is a self-funded initiative created to benefit communities through accessible and high-quality Islamic education. Our goal is not simply to teach information, but to help nurture grounded Muslims who carry knowledge with sincerity, humility, and responsibility.

We believe knowledge without character is incomplete. Memorisation without understanding is weak. Certificates without standards hold little value. For this reason, we place strong importance on tarbiyah, discipline, consistency, and authentic learning.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple.

Teach with depth.

Nurture with care.

Uphold every standard with integrity.

At HB Institution, education is an amanah. A trust that must be carried properly for the benefit of both this generation and the generations to come.

Chairman’s Message

A message from the Chairman.

HB Institution was founded on a simple belief: that Islamic education should be treated with seriousness, structure, and responsibility.

Too often, people are forced to choose between knowledge with little structure or structure with very little depth. We built HB Institution to bring both together properly.

Whether it is a child learning their first Arabic letters, an adult returning to the Quran, or a student pursuing hifz and ijazah, every student deserves qualified teachers, clear progression, honest assessment, and standards that actually mean something.

We believe sacred knowledge is an amanah. It should not be rushed, commercialised, or reduced to simple content delivery. Teaching the Quran and Islamic sciences carries responsibility, and that responsibility must be reflected in the way an institution is built, managed, and delivered.

HB Institution is a self-funded initiative under HB Foundation, established from a belief that giving back to society through beneficial knowledge is part of our duty. Every lesson, every class, and every interaction is approached with care, discipline, and sincerity to ensure quality, accessibility, and lasting impact.

All classes at HB Institution are provided completely free of charge. We believe financial circumstances should never become a barrier between a person and beneficial knowledge.

Our goal is not simply to teach information. Our goal is to help nurture grounded Muslims with character, discipline, understanding, and a stronger connection to their faith.

Where Knowledge Meets Faith.

Programs

A Complete Pathway in Islamic Education

Our programs are designed to provide structured Islamic education with clear progression, qualified teaching, and measurable development at every stage.

Whether a student is beginning their journey with the Arabic alphabet or pursuing advanced Quranic memorisation and Islamic studies, every program is built with care, discipline, and authentic methodology.

All classes are delivered free of charge as part of HB Foundation’s commitment to making beneficial knowledge accessible to communities around the world.

Our focus is not simply on teaching information, but on helping students build confidence, understanding, discipline, and a lasting connection to faith.

Our Programs

Five structured pathways.

Program 01

Quran and Tajweed

Structured Quran learning from beginner to advanced levels — including Arabic letter recognition, fluent recitation, tajweed rules, pronunciation correction, and guided recitation with qualified teachers.

  • Beginner Quran reading
  • Tajweed correction
  • Fluent recitation development
  • One-to-one sessions
  • Small group classes
  • Ijazah preparation pathways
Program 02

Hifz Program

A structured memorisation pathway focused on strong retention, consistency, revision, and accountability. Students progress through carefully monitored memorisation plans with regular testing and revision systems to ensure long-term retention rather than short-term memorisation.

  • Full-time Hifz
  • Part-time Hifz
  • Revision-focused tracks
  • Intensive memorisation programs
  • Retention testing
  • Juz-by-juz progression assessments
Program 03

Islamic Sciences

Structured study of core Islamic sciences taught through authentic sources and guided learning. Programs are designed for different age groups and levels of understanding, helping students build strong Islamic foundations with clarity and balance.

  • Tafsir
  • Hadith
  • Aqeedah
  • Fiqh
  • Seerah
  • Islamic manners and character
Program 04

Arabic Language

Arabic language support designed to strengthen Quranic understanding, pronunciation, vocabulary, and reading ability.

  • Beginner Arabic
  • Quranic Arabic
  • Reading and writing
  • Vocabulary development
  • Conversation support
  • Grammar foundations
Program 05

Specialised Programs

Additional programs designed to support different communities and educational needs.

  • Islamic studies for children
  • Programs for new Muslims
  • Teacher training pathways
  • Tarbiyah-focused classes
  • Parent support initiatives
  • Youth development programs

Every student at HB Institution is guided through a structured learning journey supported by assessment, reporting, and qualified supervision to ensure genuine progress and meaningful outcomes.

Framework

Our Academic Framework

Every standard at HB Institution is designed to be implemented, monitored, and upheld with consistency.

We believe Islamic education should be delivered with the same seriousness, accountability, and structure expected from any respected academic institution — while remaining firmly grounded in faith, tarbiyah, and authentic methodology.

Our academic framework is built to ensure students receive not only knowledge, but also guidance, discipline, measurable progression, and proper educational support throughout their journey.

Safeguarding

The wellbeing and protection of students remains a priority across all programs and learning environments.

  • Cameras required for one-to-one sessions involving minors
  • No unauthorised private communication channels
  • Structured supervision and safeguarding procedures
  • Parent involvement and reporting systems
  • Welfare-focused learning environment

Assessment

Students are assessed carefully to ensure appropriate placement, progression, and genuine understanding.

  • Entry assessment before placement
  • Defined learning pathways and pass standards
  • Regular progress evaluations
  • Retention-based testing for Hifz students
  • Revision and competency reviews before advancement

Reporting

Clear reporting systems are maintained to ensure accountability, transparency, and continuous progress monitoring.

  • Daily class reports
  • Monthly student progress reports
  • Parent communication and review meetings
  • Attendance and performance tracking
  • Escalation procedures for academic concerns

Teaching Standards

Every class is structured to maintain quality, focus, and meaningful educational outcomes.

  • Qualified teachers with defined teaching responsibilities
  • Structured curriculum and progression plans
  • Small Hifz class ratios for focused supervision
  • Age-appropriate learning pathways
  • Sessions matched according to student level and intensity
  • Consistent oversight and academic review systems

At HB Institution, standards are not treated as aspirations. They are part of the responsibility we carry towards every student, parent, and community we serve.

Governance

Built on Responsibility, Accountability, and Trust

HB Institution operates under a structured governance framework designed to protect the integrity of the institution, maintain academic standards, and ensure every decision remains aligned with our mission, values, and responsibility before Allah.

We believe Islamic education should never operate without accountability, oversight, or clear standards. For this reason, governance at HB Institution is not treated as a formality. It is part of the foundation upon which the institution is built.

Our systems are designed to ensure transparency, consistency, safeguarding, academic integrity, and responsible leadership across every level of the institution.

What We Stand Behind

  1. Every teacher at HB Institution operates under defined policies, professional expectations, and clear educational responsibilities.
  2. Every student is assessed before placement to ensure they enter the correct learning pathway according to their level and needs.
  3. Every student’s progress is monitored through structured reporting, assessments, and regular academic review.
  4. Every complaint, concern, or safeguarding matter is handled through a formal process with accountability and timely response.
  5. Every certificate issued by HB Institution represents verified progression, assessment, and genuine attainment rather than simple participation.
  6. Every program is reviewed to ensure it remains aligned with authentic Islamic methodology, educational quality, and the long-term development of students.
Leadership and Oversight

HB Institution separates operational management, academic supervision, and institutional oversight to maintain clarity, accountability, and proper governance.

Senior leadership regularly reviews:

  • Student progression and retention
  • Academic performance and assessment standards
  • Teacher performance and class quality
  • Safeguarding and welfare procedures
  • Parent and student satisfaction
  • Institutional development and quality assurance
